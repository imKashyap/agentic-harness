# Itinerary Planning Guidelines & Framework

When crafting a travel itinerary, apply these core principles to ensure the proposal is delightful, realistic, and stress-free:

## 1. Geographic Clustering (The Hub Rule)

- Never plan activities that criss-cross the city in a single day. Group morning, afternoon, and evening sights within the same district or transit corridor.
- Limit cross-town transit to once per day (usually morning departure or evening return).

## 2. Daily Energy Pacing (3-Block Rhythm)

- **Morning (09:00 - 12:30)**: Peak energy window. Schedule major cultural landmarks, temples, or museums that require high walking volume before midday crowds arrive.
- **Midday & Lunch (12:30 - 14:30)**: Dedicated break. Anchor lunch near the morning cluster. Allow at least 60-90 minutes of seated downtime.
- **Afternoon (14:30 - 17:30)**: Moderate exploration. Scenic neighborhoods, boutique shopping, parks, or tea houses.
- **Evening (18:00 - 21:30)**: Atmospheric immersion. Night markets, illuminated gardens, fine dining, or river walks.

## 3. The 70/30 Rule for Spontaneity

- Plan only 70% of each day. Leave 30% of time open as buffer for spontaneous alleyway discoveries, transit delays, or simply sitting in a café.
- Over-scheduled trips cause tourist fatigue and diminished appreciation.

## 4. Seasonal & Daylight Optimization

- In winter/late autumn (e.g. November), sunset occurs earlier (around 16:45 - 17:00). Prioritize outdoor sights and photography before 15:30.
- In summer, avoid open outdoor temple grounds between 12:00 and 15:00 to prevent heat exhaustion.
