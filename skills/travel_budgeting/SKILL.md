# Travel Budgeting Principles & Cost Management

When preparing or reviewing trip budgets, adhere to the following financial frameworks:

## 1. Cost Architecture (Fixed vs. Variable)

- **Fixed Costs (Committed early)**:
  - Long-haul transportation / flights
  - Accommodation (hotel/ryokan rooms)
  - Pre-booked rail passes (e.g. JR Pass, regional passes)
- **Variable Costs (Daily in-destination)**:
  - Food & dining: Allocate ~40% for casual meals and ~60% for special regional culinary experiences.
  - Local transit: Metro, buses, and occasional taxis.
  - Museum admissions and experiential activities (tea ceremonies, cooking classes).

## 2. The 15% Contingency Rule

- Always append a 15% buffer onto total estimated expenditures.
- This covers unexpected expenses such as bad weather taxi rides, pharmacy visits, baggage surcharges, or impulse souvenir purchases.

## 3. Hidden Cost Watchlist

- **Tourist City Taxes**: Many global cities (e.g. Kyoto, Paris, Rome, Venice) charge local municipal lodging taxes per person per night at checkout.
- **Foreign Transaction Fees**: Remind travelers to utilize credit cards with 0% foreign transaction fees and ATM cards that refund international withdrawal surcharges.
- **Tipping Norms**: Emphasize destination-specific etiquette (e.g., tipping is not practiced and can even be considered rude in Japan; standard in the US; modest service round-up in Europe).
